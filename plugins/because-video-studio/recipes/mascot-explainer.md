# Recipe · mascot explainer (45-75 s)

A character tells a parent, customer or buyer what the product does and why it matters. Proven on a mascot parent-explainer
(07-10-2026: 60 s, 3 formats, v4 after 3 review rounds, $1.72 FAL). Long film: keep the shot-list stop.

**Use when:** "explainer", "how it works", "for parents/customers", a brochure or site to turn into a story.

## Fills these brief fields
- Duration 60 s · formats 9:16 master, 1:1, 16:9 · voice: ElevenLabs via FAL (eleven-v4, word timestamps); Aman picks the voice by A/B
- Medium: code (2D canvas + Three.js for the character), FAL for concept stills only.

## Order (each step gated as noted)
1. Facts: every number from the client's own document, with page numbers. Conflicts between site and brochure go to Aman in one line.
2. Character: 2-3 FAL concept stills against the premium bar (L-104). Aman picks. Then rig it in code against the concept, side by side.
3. Script: 7 beats tagged to the audience's 3-4 real questions. Voice A/B, Aman picks.
4. Timeline: every cue pinned to a spoken word (timeline.mjs pattern: EV and HOPS tables). Picture and sound both read it.
5. Shot list stop (over 30 s), then build, check, ship.

## Structure (60 s)
Hook with a problem the viewer feels (0-4 s, works muted) · who we are · what we believe · how a session works · the ladder of levels ·
proof (numbers, alumni) · call to action with the URL.

## Blocks
hop, squash, softSpring/softTrack (camera follows the character's rest), wordCaptions, pill, prop, lockup.
Character code: build the rig fresh for each brand (expressions, idle loop); the studio's first rig is not shipped.

## Watch for (each cost a review round on the first mascot film)
- Character cut off in 16:9 and 1:1: check.mjs edges, every format.
- Camera locked to a bouncing character: follow the rest position, film.smooth on a '_cam' probe.
- Springs too subtle to feel: visible squash/stretch, antenna or head follow-through.
- Landing sound on the old time after a retime: sync entries for every hop.
- Character passing through props: add a collision item to the audit (the film's audit.mjs '!' items).
