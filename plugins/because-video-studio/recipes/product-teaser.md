# Recipe · product teaser (8-12 s)

A premium product moment on a beat grid. Proven on a product teaser (07-10-2026, 3 review rounds, $2.90 FAL).

**Use when:** one product, one or two features, a reel or ad slot. **Not for:** explaining how something works (mascot-explainer).

## Fills these brief fields
- Duration 10 s · formats 9:16 master, 1:1, 16:9 · music: synthesize on a 120 BPM grid (a beat every 0.5 s)
- Medium: mixed. FAL stills give the material look; code owns every timed event. Kling only where an object must physically move.

## Structure (on the 120 BPM grid)
| Beat | Time | Job | Device that worked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-2.5 s | The product's signature detail changes state on every beat | Off plate + glow plate aligned to 0 px, glow masked per part, one part per beat |
| Feature 1 | 2.5-4 s | Macro of the detail | 1-1.5 s of a Kling push-in, code glow pulse on the beat |
| Feature 2 | 4-6 s | A number or mode, shown not said | Code-drawn ring segments lighting one per beat over a still |
| Proof | 6-7.5 s | The everyday action (plug in, open, tap) | Kling clip cut before it drifts, then the seated still under the click |
| Lockup | 7.5-10 s | Brand | bloom() into the lockup, logo on the downbeat (heavy spring), tagline 0.5 s later |

## Blocks
cover, band (captions in the top band: Reels UI covers the bottom), line, bloom, lockup, flash, clipFrame.

## Sound
Restrained set only: tick per switch, thump on cuts, hit on the seat, final on the lockup downbeat. -14 LUFS via ship.

## Known limits (do not rediscover)
- The image model adds or removes light in an edit; it will not move a part (asked twice, unchanged). Design motion around light.
- Kling clips drift after about 3 s (darkening, parts showing through). Use the clean seconds only, at most 1.8x speed.
- Ask for 3:2 stills and compose the 9:16 frame in code; asking for 9:16 rotated the product.
- Price before generating: stills about $0.15 each, Kling v3 Pro about $0.70 per 5 s. Cap $5 unless the brief says more.
