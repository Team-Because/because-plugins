# Recipe · festive wish (12-20 s)

A brand's greeting for a festival (Navratri, Diwali, Eid, Christmas, New Year). Built 09-10-2026 from the festive round
and lessons L-103, L-111, L-112. Not yet shipped end to end: the first film on this recipe updates it.

**Use when:** "wish", "greeting", "festive post", any festival name. **Not for:** a festive offer or sale (product-teaser plus a price card).

## Fills these brief fields
- Duration 15 s · formats 9:16 master, 1:1 · music: synthesize, festival-true rhythm (dhol 6/8 for Garba, soft strings or bells for Diwali)
- Medium: motion graphics in code (L-112). FAL only for a texture or a still the code cannot draw, never for people at the festival.

## The idea comes first (pitch 3, Aman picks)
The product becomes the festive object (L-103). Shape the product's form into the festival's signature shape, never drop the product
into a festival photo. Examples of the move: a wire's coil drawn as the Garba circle; cables lighting up as the lamps of a diya row;
a bike's light trail writing the greeting. Each pitch is one still plus one line. At least two pitches use the proven genre (real product
material in premium light, type and springs); an illustrated character or crowd concept only with a one-line ceiling warning
(taste 24, L-113: rejected as childish 4 times).
Check every pitch: would this object really be here? If not, it is a graphic, not a scene (L-112).

## Structure
| Beat | Time (15 s) | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-2 s | The product's form, alone, makes its first move. Frame 0 already shows it. |
| Build | 2-9 s | The form becomes the festive object, one transformation per beat on the music's downbeats |
| Peak | 9-12 s | The festive object complete, lit, alive (the poster frame) |
| Wish | 12-15 s | Greeting in the brand's voice (client CLAUDE.md line), logo lockup, hold allowed here only |

## Blocks
line (greeting), bloom (lighting moments), lockup, softTrack (the transformation paths), flash, wordCaptions if a voice line is used.

## Sound
Real-sounding rhythm, the festival's instrument family, a sub hit on the peak. The music can drop out for one beat before the peak.
No novelty effects (L-102).

## Watch for
- The festival cliche parade (lamps, sticks, rangoli, fireworks as decoration). One festive object, made from the product.
- Greeting text: brand voice, sentence case, the client's own line if it has one.
- Regional audience: check spellings of the festival name and greeting with the client file, never guess a local-language line.
