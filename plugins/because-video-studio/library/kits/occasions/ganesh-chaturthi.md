# Occasion kit · Ganesh Chaturthi

Client-neutral. Built 12-10-2026. A brief accelerator, not a template: the reference and the picked concept decide the shots.

## The occasion
Ten days for Ganesha, the remover of obstacles and the god of beginnings, ending in Anant Chaturdashi immersion. Biggest in Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka, Telangana and Gujarat.

## The cliche to avoid
Ganesha drawn as a cartoon holding the product. Brands are criticised for putting a deity next to a product. The occasion is new beginnings: the product starts something (a new home, a first car, a new class).

## Colours (festival accents; the client's brand colours always lead)
Saffron #F28C28, vermilion #D7263D, marigold #F5A623, cream #FFF4DC.

## Motifs
Modak, durva grass, marigold garlands, the dhol-tasha beat, the pandal crowd. Never animate the deity; show the celebration around him.

## Date rule
Bhadrapada Shukla Chaturthi, August or September. 2026: 14-09-2026 (Monday). 2027: 04-09-2027 (Saturday). The festival runs 10 days from that date.
