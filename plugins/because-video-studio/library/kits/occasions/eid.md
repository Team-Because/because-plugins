# Occasion kit · Eid (Id-ul-Fitr and Id-ul-Zuha)

Client-neutral. Built 12-10-2026. A brief accelerator, not a template: the reference and the picked concept decide the shots.

## The occasion
Id-ul-Fitr ends Ramzan's month of fasting: new clothes, sevaiyan, Eidi gifts to children, family visits. Id-ul-Zuha (Bakrid) follows about 70 days later.

## The cliche to avoid
A crescent moon and lantern stamped over the logo, and the colour green used as shorthand. The product becomes part of the visit: the outfit, the sweet dish, the Eidi in a child's hand.

## Colours (festival accents; the client's brand colours always lead)
Deep emerald #0F5E4B, lantern gold #E2B04A, night blue #13213C, ivory #FAF3E3.

## Motifs
The moon-sighting moment, lanterns, sevaiyan, embroidery, the iftar table during Ramzan. Respect the fast in Ramzan content (no food close-ups at midday postings).

## Date rule
Lunar, moves about 11 days earlier each year, and the final date depends on the moon sighting (the DoPT order says the date can change at short notice). Id-ul-Fitr 2026: 21-03-2026; 2027: 10-03-2027. Id-ul-Zuha 2026: 27-05-2026; 2027: 17-05-2027. Never lock a post to the day: schedule it for the confirmed sighting.

Sources (checked 12-10-2026):
- 2026: DoPT O.M. F.No.12/2/2023-JCA dated 03-07-2025 (read from the PDF copy at https://www.staffnews.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Holidays-to-be-observed-in-Central-Government-Offices-during-the-year-2026.pdf; the dopt.gov.in file returns a soft 404)
