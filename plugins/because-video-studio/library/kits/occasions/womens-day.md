# Occasion kit · International Women's Day

Client-neutral. Built 12-10-2026. A brief accelerator, not a template: the reference and the picked concept decide the shots.

## The occasion
8 March, a United Nations observance (https://www.un.org/en/observances/womens-day). Brands use it to talk about women at work, money, health and safety.

## The cliche to avoid
Pink, flowers, "superwoman" and a discount on products for women. Audiences now call this out as empty. A real woman's real story, or a real policy change from the brand, with numbers.

## Colours (festival accents; the client's brand colours always lead)
No festive palette: use the brand's own colours. If an accent is needed, the UN theme colour for the year, checked on the UN page.

## Motifs
Real people and real work, a fact with a source, the change the brand made. No stock-photo boardroom.

Every sector; employers use it for hiring and culture content.
