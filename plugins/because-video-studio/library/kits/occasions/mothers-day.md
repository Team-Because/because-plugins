# Occasion kit · Mother's Day

Client-neutral. Built 12-10-2026. A brief accelerator, not a template: the reference and the picked concept decide the shots.

## The occasion
A commercial observance: India follows the US date, the second Sunday of May. No government notification exists for it.

## The cliche to avoid
A mother serving food to everyone and sacrifice as the only virtue. Show mothers as people with their own life, and the product as a gift to her, not to the household.

## Colours (festival accents; the client's brand colours always lead)
Soft rose #F2B5C0, warm cream #FFF2E2, sage #9DB89A, plus the brand's own colour.

## Motifs
Handwritten notes, a phone call home, gift unboxing, an old photo coming to life.
