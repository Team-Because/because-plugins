# Occasion kit · Raksha Bandhan

Client-neutral. Built 12-10-2026. A brief accelerator, not a template: the reference and the picked concept decide the shots.

## The occasion
Sisters tie a rakhi on brothers' wrists; brothers promise protection and give a gift. Today it covers any sibling pair.

## The cliche to avoid
The protective brother and grateful sister. That framing is dated and draws criticism. Siblings as equals and teasing as love; the product is the gift that is actually wanted.

## Colours (festival accents; the client's brand colours always lead)
Rakhi red #C8102E, thread gold #D4A017, soft peach #FFD6C2, teal #1C8C8C.

## Motifs
The thread being tied (a close-up of hands), sweets, the sibling argument, gift unboxing, sending a rakhi to a sibling who lives far away.
