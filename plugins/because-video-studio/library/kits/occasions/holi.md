# Occasion kit · Holi

Client-neutral. Built 12-10-2026. A brief accelerator, not a template: the reference and the picked concept decide the shots.

## The occasion
The festival of colours at the end of winter: thrown colour, water, sweets (gujiya), bhang, music. Holika Dahan (the bonfire) is the night before.

## The cliche to avoid
A splash of powder exploding over the logo. Stock-photo faces covered in colour. The product becomes the colour or survives it: the shirt that washes clean, the phone that stays dry, the sweet on the plate.

## Colours (festival accents; the client's brand colours always lead)
Magenta #E4007C, turmeric #FFC20E, leaf green #3DB54A, sky blue #29ABE2, on white.

## Motifs
Powder clouds (build them in code: seeded particles, never a stock splash), water balloons, gujiya, white clothes that become colour. Keep skin and faces respectful, no forced colouring.

## Date rule
Phalguna Purnima (full moon), February or March. 2026: Holika Dahan 03-03-2026, Holi 04-03-2026. 2027: Holika Dahan 22-03-2027, Holi 23-03-2027.
