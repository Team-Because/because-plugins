# Occasion kit · Onam

Client-neutral. Built 12-10-2026. A brief accelerator, not a template: the reference and the picked concept decide the shots.

## The occasion
Kerala's harvest festival over ten days, ending on Thiruvonam: the pookalam flower carpet, the sadya feast on a banana leaf, boat races, and King Mahabali's yearly visit.

## The cliche to avoid
A kathakali face and a houseboat on the logo. The product becomes part of the sadya or the pookalam: it sits on the leaf, it is laid out in petals.

## Colours (festival accents; the client's brand colours always lead)
Kasavu cream #F4E9D0, kasavu gold #C9A227, banana-leaf green #3C8D2F, marigold #F5A623.

## Motifs
The pookalam (build it in code: concentric petal rings, seeded), the sadya leaf seen from above, the snake boat oars in rhythm, the off-white and gold kasavu saree.

## Date rule
Thiruvonam falls in Chingam (Malayalam month), August or September. 2026: 26-08-2026 (Wednesday). 2027: 12-09-2027 (Sunday). Celebrations start about 10 days before.
