# Occasion kit · Independence Day

Client-neutral. Built 12-10-2026. A brief accelerator, not a template: the reference and the picked concept decide the shots.

## The occasion
15 August 1947. Flag hoisting, the Prime Minister's speech from the Red Fort, kites over Delhi.

## The cliche to avoid
Tricolour gradients and a flag waving behind the logo, plus a percentage-off sale. The national flag may not be used for commercial purposes (Flag Code of India 2002, 2.1(i), read with the Emblems and Names (Prevention of Improper Use) Act 1950, s.3; any picture of the flag counts under the Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act 1971, Explanation 2): https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/flagcodeofindia_070214.pdf. The product's own idea of freedom (a choice, a first, a self-made business) carries the day instead.

## Colours (festival accents; the client's brand colours always lead)
Saffron #FF9933, white #FFFFFF, green #138808, navy #000080 are the flag's colours: use them as separate accents, never assembled into a flag, and never with the Ashoka Chakra.

## Motifs
Kites, the morning parade, school uniforms, a first-generation story. Studio rule: no national flag, emblem or Ashoka Chakra in any client video.
