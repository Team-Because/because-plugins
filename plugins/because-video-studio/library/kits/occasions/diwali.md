# Occasion kit · Diwali (Deepavali)

Client-neutral. Built 12-10-2026. A brief accelerator, not a template: the reference and the picked concept decide the shots.

## The occasion
The festival of lights: lamps, new clothes, gifting, home cleaning and buying (gold, vehicles, electronics, homes). The single biggest sale season in India; Dhanteras two days before is a buying day in itself.

## The cliche to avoid
Diyas, rangoli and fireworks parading across the frame with the logo stamped on the end. Every brand runs it, so none of them is remembered. The product becomes the festive object instead: the product lights the room, the product is the gift, the product is what the family gathers around.

## Colours (festival accents; the client's brand colours always lead)
Warm gold #E8A33D, deep maroon #6B1E2E, lamp orange #FF8A1F, night indigo #1B1A3A. Secondary accents only; the client's brand colours lead.

## Motifs
Light on a dark room, a single flame, warm bokeh, gift wrap, the new-purchase moment. Avoid firecrackers as a hero image (several states restrict them; many brands now avoid them).

## Date rule
Kartika Amavasya (new moon of Kartika), so October or November. 2026: 08-11-2026 (Sunday), Naraka Chaturdasi also 08-11-2026, Govardhan Puja 09-11-2026, Bhai Duj 11-11-2026. 2027: 29-10-2027 (Friday), Naraka Chaturdasi 28-10-2027, Govardhan Puja 30-10-2027, Bhai Duj 31-10-2027.

## Lead time (studio default, not data)
Brief 4-6 weeks out; sale creatives often go live 2 weeks before Dhanteras.

## Sectors that use it
Retail, D2C, auto and real estate push offers; services brands send a greeting. Industrial and B2B: a thank-you to dealers and partners.
