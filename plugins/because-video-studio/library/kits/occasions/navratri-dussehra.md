# Occasion kit · Navratri and Dussehra (Vijaya Dashami)

Client-neutral. Built 12-10-2026. A brief accelerator, not a template: the reference and the picked concept decide the shots.

## The occasion
Nine nights of the goddess (Sharad Navratri), then Dussehra, the victory of good over evil. Garba and dandiya nights in Gujarat and western India, Durga Puja in the east, Ramlila and the burning of Ravana in the north.

## The cliche to avoid
A goddess illustration or a Ravana effigy with the logo, and nine colour-of-the-day posts that look the same as every other brand's. The product becomes part of the night: the outfit, the ride to the garba ground, the snack between rounds.

## Colours (festival accents; the client's brand colours always lead)
Vermilion #E23D28, marigold #F5A623, deep green #1F6B3A, royal blue #23408E. Navratri has a colour for each day; check the year's list, it changes with the start weekday.

## Motifs
Circular motion (garba rounds), dandiya sticks, mirror work, marigold, the drum beat. Do not draw a deity as a mascot or a cartoon.

## Date rule
Navratri is the nine nights before Dussehra (Ashvin Shukla 1 to 9). 2026: Mahashtami 19-10-2026, Mahanavami 20-10-2026, Dussehra 20-10-2026 (Tuesday). 2027: Saptami 06-10-2027, Mahashtami 07-10-2027, Mahanavami 08-10-2027, Dussehra 09-10-2027 (Saturday). The first-night date is not in either order: confirm it from the state list or the client before dating a post.

## Lead time (studio default, not data)
Brief 4 weeks out; garba-night content runs daily for 9 days, so plan a series, not one film.

## Sectors that use it
Fashion, food and beverage, two-wheelers and events lead. Gujarat clients treat it as the main festival of the year.
