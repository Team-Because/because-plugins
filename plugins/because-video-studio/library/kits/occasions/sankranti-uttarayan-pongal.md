# Occasion kit · Makar Sankranti, Uttarayan and Pongal

Client-neutral. Built 12-10-2026. A brief accelerator, not a template: the reference and the picked concept decide the shots.

## The occasion
The sun enters Capricorn: the harvest festival under many names. Uttarayan in Gujarat (two days of kite flying on rooftops), Pongal in Tamil Nadu (rice boiled over in a new pot), Lohri the night before in Punjab, Magh Bihu in Assam.

## The cliche to avoid
A generic kite on a blue sky with the logo as the kite. The kite fight is the better idea: the product cuts through, or stays up when others fall.

## Colours (festival accents; the client's brand colours always lead)
Sky blue #4FB3E8, kite pink #E8467C, kite yellow #FFC93C, sugarcane green #4C9A2A, pot terracotta #B5552B.

## Motifs
Rooftop kites and manja (string), til-gul and chikki, the overflowing Pongal pot, kolam, sugarcane.

## Date rule
Solar, so almost fixed: 14-01 (sometimes 15-01). 2026: Makar Sankranti and Pongal 14-01-2026. 2027: Makar Sankranti and Magh Bihu 14-01-2027 (Thursday), Pongal 15-01-2027 (Friday). Uttarayan is the Makar Sankranti date in Gujarat, with Vasi Uttarayan the next day.
