# Occasion kit · New Year

Client-neutral. Built 12-10-2026. A brief accelerator, not a template: the reference and the picked concept decide the shots.

## The occasion
31 December night and 1 January. Parties, countdowns, resolutions, year-in-review posts.

## The cliche to avoid
A clock striking twelve with fireworks and "Happy New Year" in gold script. The idea is a fresh start: what the product makes possible next year, or an honest year-in-review with real numbers.

## Colours (festival accents; the client's brand colours always lead)
Champagne gold #D8B26E, midnight #0D0F1E, silver #C9CED6, one bright accent from the brand.

## Motifs
The countdown, confetti, a resolution list, a calendar turning, the year's numbers.

## Date rule
Fixed: 01-01 every year. 2027: 01-01-2027 (Friday).

Sources (checked 12-10-2026):
- 2027: DoPT O.M. dated 16-07-2026 as circulated by the Department of Posts (https://utilities.cept.gov.in/dop/pdfbind.ashx?id=15435)

## Lead time (studio default, not data)
Brief 3 weeks out; a year-in-review needs the client's numbers by mid-December.

## Sectors that use it
Every sector; fitness, education and finance own the resolution angle.
