# Occasion kit · Christmas

Client-neutral. Built 12-10-2026. A brief accelerator, not a template: the reference and the picked concept decide the shots.

## The occasion
25 December. Midnight mass, cake, stars on balconies, Santa for children, and the start of the year-end holiday season in metros, Goa, Kerala and the north-east.

## The cliche to avoid
Snow and a red Santa hat on the logo, in a country where most cities are 25 degrees. The product becomes the gift or the reason people come together.

## Colours (festival accents; the client's brand colours always lead)
Pine green #1E5631, berry red #B3001B, star gold #F2C14E, warm white #FFF8EC.

## Motifs
The paper star lantern on balconies, plum cake, fairy lights, Secret Santa at work, the gift exchange.

## Date rule
Fixed: 25-12 every year. Christmas Eve 24-12. 2026: Friday. 2027: Saturday.

Brief 4 weeks out; it is usually paired with New Year in one campaign.

Gifting, food and bakery, retail, hospitality.
