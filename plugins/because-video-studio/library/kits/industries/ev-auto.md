# Industry kit · EV, two-wheelers and auto

Client-neutral. Built 12-10-2026 from the studio's first two EV films (an x-ray reel and a chrome-controls reel, 10 s each).

## What the viewer must get
The product is real and premium, one or two numbers that matter (range, acceleration, charging), how to buy or prebook.

## Visuals used so far (options, not a template: the reference and concept decide)
- The maker's own renders (side view, hero portrait, part close-ups). Never invent a part or redraw a dashboard UI.
- Route C looks: x-ray of the real silhouette (looks/xray.md), chrome feature objects with one brand glow
  (looks/chrome-glow.md).
- Features as objects that switch on per beat (ride modes, gears, charging), with the fact as a caption.
- Product parts in macro with premium light; logo lockup on a gradient.

## Compliance (check every time)
- Range: say the test standard and "up to" exactly as the maker's site words it (e.g. IDC range "up to ... under ideal
  riding conditions"). Never a bare range number.
- Prices only with the offer's condition (e.g. "for the first N customers"), and only if the brief asks.
- Specs only from the maker's site, re-checked before each video (many EV sites render specs in the browser: use
  Playwright, not curl).

## Formats
9:16 Reel master; 1:1 and 16:9 from the same timeline when the brief asks.
