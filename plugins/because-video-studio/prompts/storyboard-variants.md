# Storyboard variants (pick a direction before the shot list)

Source: Rexan Wong's workflow, steps 4-5 (https://x.com/rexan_wong/status/2103707054108299437).
Why: the first idea is rarely the best one, and fixing a storyboard is far cheaper than fixing a render.
Three variants means you choose a direction instead of polishing whatever came first.

Use between step 05 (style guide approved) and the shot list.

```
Context you have: brand/ (logo, colours, fonts, brand.json), brand/assets (real product screenshots),
the client CLAUDE.md (positioning, facts), refs/ and docs/style_guide.md (the look), brief.md (the job).
My braindump: [2-5 lines on how I see the video, the feeling, the one thing it must say].

1. Pitch 3 storyboard variants for a [DURATION]s [FORMAT] video, all in the approved style:
   - each with a one-line concept, the hook (first 2 s), the beat structure, and how it ends;
   - each with ONE still frame of its key moment (render it, don't describe it);
   - make them genuinely different (e.g. product-first, story-first, data-first), not three versions of one idea.
2. Put the 3 stills side by side in docs/variants.png and the pitches in docs/variants.md.
3. Wait for me to pick one. Then write docs/shotlist.md for that variant only, and render one still per shot
   (tools/stills.mjs) before anything moves.
```

Rules
- Every variant uses only real assets and site-verified facts (client CLAUDE.md). No invented features or numbers.
- Never mix clients: one client's brand and refs only.
