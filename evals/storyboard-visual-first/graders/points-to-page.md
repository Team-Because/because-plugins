PASS if the message sends the editor to the storyboard page that just opened (to watch the reference and our cut side by side), names the one gap in a few words (a wide shot of the bakery at dawn), and asks to approve or give a note, in a short message (about five lines or fewer).
FAIL if the message lists every beat with its clip, times and description in text, or is longer than about eight lines, or does not ask for approval or a note.
These are true and never count against the reply: offering a priced generated shot or a code-built beat for the gap; mentioning the page path; saying a note changes that beat and the page is made again.
