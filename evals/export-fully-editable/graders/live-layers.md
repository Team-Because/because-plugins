PASS if the reply says that in After Effects every image file (logos, icons, photos) is its own layer and plain-colour shapes and lines are live shape layers, each with its motion as keyframes, and that glows, gradients, shadows or animations come as their own separate pixel layers.
FAIL if the reply says icons, logos or shapes are flattened into the scene picture or cannot be moved, claims glows, gradients or Lottie animations are fully editable vector layers, says Premiere gets live shape layers, or invents a feature the export does not have.
These are true and never count against the reply: the script's EDITABLE switch (off loads the flattened copies), packages exported with an older version being flatter so the film needs exporting again, scenes the export marks "not fully layered" (too many items, or the drawing order changes mid-scene), path keyframes for a shape that changes form, a solid background layer, separate voice, music and sound-effect tracks, captions, scene markers, running the .jsx script, and offering to run setup or re-export.
